Naturalness

−100 is much less natural, 0 is about as natural, and +100 is much more natural than the literal. For calibration, query-fragment wording may sit near −80, similarly fluent wording near 0, and an ordinary conversational question near +80.

Communicative salience

−100 is much less focused, 0 about as focused, and +100 much more focused on a plausible human information need. Exhaustive structural detail may sit near −80, equivalent focus near 0, and a concise statement of the likely need near +80.

Room for interpretation

−100 is much more constrained, 0 about as open, and +100 much more open to interpretation. Unsupported narrowing may sit near −80, equivalent scope near 0, and materially underspecified wording near +80. Neither direction is inherently better.
