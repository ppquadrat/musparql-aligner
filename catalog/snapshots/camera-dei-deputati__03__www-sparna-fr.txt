        <h1 id="dati.camera.it&#39;s-sparnatural-instance-:-a-query-builder-for-the-italian-chamber-of-deputies-title" class="post__title p-name title">dati.camera.itâs Sparnatural instance : a query builder for the Italian Chamber of Deputies </h1>
        <div class="post__meta">
            <p>
                <time class="post__date dt-published">17 Jul 2025</time>
                
                <span aria-label="author">&middot;</span> <span class="post__author p-author">by <span>Marie Muller</span>
                </span>
                
                
                <span aria-label="in category">&middot;</span> <span class="post__tags p-category">open data,  linked open data,  Sparnatural,  ontology</span>
                
            </p>
        </div>
    </div>


    <div class="post__content e-content markdown" itemprop="articleBody">
        <p class="lead">While universities, research centers, and foundations have no difficulty using direct SPARQL queries, the use of Sparnatural is the solution that offers everyone the ability to explore the data</p>
<p>The open data portal of the Italian Chamber of Deputies (<a href="http://dati.camera.it/" title="http://dati.camera.it/">dati.camera.it</a>) offers public data on parliamentary activity in an open and reusable format for citizens, developers, journalists, scholars and public administrations. The published data concern, for instance, the activity of parliamentarians, parliamentary groups and organs of the Chamber, the composition and offices of the government, bills and parliamentary acts in general, sessions and minutes, results of electronic votes, etc. The data are organised according to a semantic model, formally represented by <a href="https://dati.camera.it/en/ontology-chamber-deputies" title="https://dati.camera.it/en/ontology-chamber-deputies">the ontology of the Chamber of Deputies (OCD)</a>.</p>
<p><a href="/assets/posts-images/dati-ontology.png"><img src="/assets/posts-images/dati-ontology.png" alt=""></a></p>
<p>The portal offers the ability to export data as linked open data (LOD) collections, in RDF format, organized into thematic datasets ...</p>
<p><a href="/assets/posts-images/dati-themes.png"><img src="/assets/posts-images/dati-themes.png" alt=""></a></p>
<p>... or to query open data directly, exposing <a href="https://dati.camera.it/sparql" title="https://dati.camera.it/sparql">a dedicated SPARQL</a> endpoint. All data are provided under <a href="https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/" title="https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/">a Creative Commons By license</a>. Knowing the OCD ontology, it is then possible to write SPARQL queries and obtain the desired data, to be used for studies and research.</p>
<p><a href="/assets/posts-images/dati-queries.png"><img src="/assets/posts-images/dati-queries.png" alt=""></a></p>
<p><a href="https://dati.camera.it/lodlive/?http://dati.camera.it/ocd/" title="https://dati.camera.it/lodlive/?http://dati.camera.it/ocd/">A Lodlive graph</a> is also available in the menu, where you can enter the URI of a resource and visualize all of its properties and relationships with other entities.</p>
<p><a href="/assets/posts-images/dati-lodlive.png"><img src="/assets/posts-images/dati-lodlive.png" alt=""></a></p>
<p>While universities, research centers, and foundations have no difficulty accessing the open data, via direct SPARQL queries, this is not so true for ordinary citizens. The use of Sparnaâs <a href="https://sparnatural.eu/" title="https://sparnatural.eu/">Sparnatural  query builder</a>  is therefore the solution that offers everyone the ability to explore the data, through an intuitive interface that guides the user in composing the desired search path.</p>
<p><a href="https://dati.camera.it/sparnatural/">Dati.camera.it's Sparnatural</a> allows SPARQL queries to be constructed, visually and intuitively :</p>
<p><a href="/assets/posts-images/dati-sparnatural.png"><img src="/assets/posts-images/dati-sparnatural.png" alt=""></a></p>
<p>Aiming at querying the knowledge graph in a user-friendly way (i.e. the users donât need to know SPARQL query language to use the query builder), the tool has been highly appreciated by the users of theÂ dati.camera.itÂ portal.</p>
<p>Here some examples of queries and their results in Sparnatural (click on &quot;Toggle SPARQL query&quot; button to display the SPARQL query itself)Â :</p>
<p><strong>The list of deputies who belong to Italy's Green and Left Alliance (Alleanza Verdi e Sinistra)  parliamentary groupÂ :</strong></p>
<p><a href="/assets/posts-images/dati-query1.png"><img src="/assets/posts-images/dati-query1.png" alt=""></a></p>
<p><a href="/assets/posts-images/dati-sparql1.png"><img src="/assets/posts-images/dati-sparql1.png" alt=""></a></p>
<p><strong>The list of acts initiated by the government for the term of the First Constituent AssemblyÂ :</strong></p>
<p><a href="/assets/posts-images/dati-query2.png"><img src="/assets/posts-images/dati-query2.png" alt=""></a></p>
<p><a href="/assets/posts-images/dati-sparql2.png"><img src="/assets/posts-images/dati-sparql2.png" alt=""></a></p>
<p><strong>The Presidents of the Parliamentary Groups of the XIX Legislature of the Italian Republic</strong></p>
<p><a href="/assets/posts-images/dati-query3.png"><img src="/assets/posts-images/dati-query3.png" alt=""></a></p>
<p><a href="/assets/posts-images/dati-sparql3.png"><img src="/assets/posts-images/dati-sparql3.png" alt=""></a></p>
<p>Give it a try here : <a href="https://dati.camera.it/sparnatural/">https://dati.camera.it/sparnatural/</a></p>
<p>Another interesting implementation of Sparnatural in the context of parliamentary linked open data (see our latest blog article about <a href="https://blog.sparna.fr/2025/04/09/european-parliament-open-data-portal-a-shacl-powered-knowledge-graph/">European Parliament Open Data Portal</a>) !</p>
