Musparql is an ICF research study about reviewing natural-language questions and SPARQL queries. Taking part is voluntary. We collect your profile, expertise, language and review data to run the study and analyse the benchmark.

For the IPL workshop, you may work alone or in a self-selected reviewing group. A group’s joint submission is attributed to the pseudonymous reviewer identifiers of the people who contributed to it.

We publish benchmark questions and ambiguity or alternative data, but not your name, contact details, reviewer profile, or reviewer-linked annotations.

You may stop or withdraw at any time using the withdrawal control in Musparql, by telling the workshop facilitator, or by emailing musparql@industrycommons.net. We stop using your personal research data and delete it as explained below. Genuinely non-identifying benchmark outputs may remain. ICF keeps limited security, accountability and backup records separately under its legitimate interests.

**You can object to the records kept under legitimate interests. See Your data-protection rights below.**

Contact musparql@industrycommons.net about the study or your data rights.
