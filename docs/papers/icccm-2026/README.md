# ICCCM 2026

This folder contains the ICCCM 2026 paper and will also hold its poster and
presentation slides.

The paper develops **interpretive mediation** through HERMES Culture Knowledge
Graph challenge interactions and Musparql examples. It is a distinct scholarly
output from the MTSR Musparql workflow/benchmark paper.

The knowledge-graph source-discovery experiment is another distinct line of
work. Its evidence and conclusions remain under
[`docs/experiments/2026-08-22-kg-source-discovery-experiment.md`](../../experiments/2026-08-22-kg-source-discovery-experiment.md)
and [`docs/graph-discovery/`](../../graph-discovery/).
